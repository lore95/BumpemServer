\section{Step 2: cabling}\label{sec:walk-cabling}
Stanford guide B3, B6, B7 (pp.\,15--17). All supplies unplugged during this whole step. Full pin tables:
Sect.~\ref{sec:cabling}.

\subsection{Motor to ESCON}
The motor has one cable with 3 winding wires and 5 Hall-sensor wires. On the ESCON 70/10:
\begin{table}[H]\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
ESCON & Pin & Wire & Signal \\
\midrule
J2 Motor & 1 / 2 / 3 & red / brown / white & winding 1 / 2 / 3 \\
         & 4 & \textbf{empty} & shield \\
J3 Hall  & 1 / 2 / 3 & yellow / pink / grey & Hall 1 / 2 / 3 \\
         & 4 & green & +5\,V Hall supply \\
         & 5 & blue  & Hall GND \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
The colours are the lab's extension wires. If yours differ, go by the motor's pin numbers
(Sect.~\ref{sec:cabling}). Two modules had the blue Hall GND on J2 pin 4 and one had green and grey swapped;
both make the motor fail or pull far too hard.

\textbf{Check:} with a meter, J2 pin 1--2, 2--3, 3--1 read about 1\,$\Omega$ each (motor windings plus cable).

\subsection{Load cell to amplifier}
Load cell cable to the Futek IAA100 sensor side: 1 +excitation, 2 +signal, 3 $-$signal, 4 $-$excitation or
shield. \fillin{DYMH-103 wire colours per IAA100 terminal}

\subsection{Ground bridge}
On the tower, one small bridge joins the grounds: ESCON J5 pin 5, ESCON J6 pin 7, IAA100 GND and the motor
ground. From the bridge one \textbf{white} wire goes to the control board (Sect.~\ref{sec:ground}).

\subsection{Signal cable to the control board}
One multi-core cable per tower, five wires used. Label both ends.
\begin{table}[H]\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Wire & Tower end & Meaning \\
\midrule
\textcolor{wyellow}{\rule{1.2em}{0.6em}} yellow & ESCON J5 pin 2 (DigIN2) & enable \\
\textcolor{wblue}{\rule{1.2em}{0.6em}} blue & ESCON J6 pin 3 (AnIN2+) & setpoint + \\
\textcolor{wgreen}{\rule{1.2em}{0.6em}} green & ESCON J6 pin 4 (AnIN2$-$) & setpoint $-$ \\
\textcolor{wwhite}{\rule{1.2em}{0.6em}} white & ground bridge & ground \\
\textcolor{wbrown}{\rule{1.2em}{0.6em}} brown & IAA100 pin 5 (Vout) & force \\
pink, black & nothing & spares, taped \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
\warn{Never connect ESCON J5 pin 6 (+5\,V) or J6 pins 5--6 (analog outputs, down to $-4$\,V) to the control
board: they would damage the Teensy.}

\textbf{Check:} each wire beeps end to end; no two wires beep with each other; white beeps to ESCON J5 pin 5.
